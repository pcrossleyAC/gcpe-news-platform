**Who this is for:** Release viewers (NRMS.Viewer).

## Open a release you know

1. Type into **Find a release**, at the top of the side menu, one of:
   - its reference number, such as NEWS-00007, or just 00007;
   - its key, such as 2026HLTH0003-000008 (shown as **Key** under **Release info**);
   - its public web address.
2. Choose **Find**.

The release opens. If nothing has that number, you get **Search** results for what you typed instead.

## Search

1. Choose **Search** in the side menu.
2. Type part of the headline in **Search**, word for word (capitals don't matter), or leave it empty and use the filters in step 3.
3. To narrow it, choose a **Ministry**, **Sector** or **Type**, or a **From** and **To** date. The dates are the release date: when it went out, or for one that hasn't, its planned or scheduled time. Both days are included; leave either empty for no limit on that side. If **From** is after **To**, it says "The From date is after the To date." and doesn't search. They work on their own too: choose only a **Type** and dates, for example, to list everything of that type from those days.
4. Choose **Search**.

Typing a reference number, key or web address here opens the release directly, as **Find a release** does.

Before you search, **Search** lists the five most recently published releases.

Results include drafts, scheduled and published releases, newest first, 20 to a page. Deleted releases don't appear. If nothing matches, you see "No releases match" and your words. If any filter is chosen, choose **Clear filters** to try again without them.

Search looks at headlines only. To find a published release by other words, use the public site's own **Search** page.

A reference such as HLTH-123 also finds the releases linked to that Calendar activity. Anything that looks like this, such as COVID-19, is also searched for in headlines. The headlines are left out only when it names an activity you can see in the Calendar that has releases linked to it.
