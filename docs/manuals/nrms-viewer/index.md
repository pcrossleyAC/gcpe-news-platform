**Who this is for:** Release viewers (NRMS.Viewer).

You can open every news release, story, factsheet and advisory in the Hub: drafts, scheduled releases and published ones. You can't change them. If something needs changing, ask a release editor.

## What you can do

| Where | What you do there | Help page |
|---|---|---|
| **Releases** | See the drafts, scheduled and published releases, and what is coming up on **Forecast**. | [The release folders](manual:viewer-release-folders) |
| The release itself | Read its text, details and files, open its PDF or public page, and email yourself a copy. | [Read a release](manual:viewer-read-a-release) |
| **Find a release** and **Search** | Open a release by its number, or find it by its headline. | [Find a release](manual:viewer-find-a-release) |
| **Website** | See which releases are featured on the public site, and every change made to the site. | [What's featured where and the Website log](manual:viewer-featured-and-log) |

Release editors also see **New release**, **Actions** and **Similar published releases**. You don't. They have their own manual, the Releases editor manual.

## What you see in the side menu

At the top is **Find a release**. Under **Work** you see **Releases**, **Search** and **Website**.

You may also see **Subscribers** or **Calendar** if you have a role there. Those have their own manuals.

## Signing in

Choose **Sign in with Microsoft** and use your government Microsoft account. You need a Hub account first: if you see "Your account hasn't been set up. Contact an administrator.", ask a Core administrator to add you. Signing out of the Hub doesn't sign you out of Microsoft.

If the sign-in page also shows **User name** and **Password**, you can sign in with those instead: fill them in and choose **Sign in**.
