**Who this is for:** Release editors (NRMS.Editor).

Publishing emails people, and only the first time. Saving, approving, scheduling, correcting and unpublishing don't.

## When you publish

| What | Who or where | When |
|---|---|---|
| Public news site | The release's own page, and the home, ministry, sector, theme and tag pages that list it. Not for advisories. | Usually within a couple of minutes of the publish time. |
| News On Demand, as it happens | Active subscribers who chose As it happens and follow any of the release's ministries, sectors, themes or tags, and everyone subscribed to all news. | Soon after it publishes. |
| News On Demand, daily digest | The same people, if they chose the digest. | The next digest, at 5:00 pm BC time. |
| Media distribution lists | Every member of each list you ticked. They get the full text. | Soon after it publishes. |
| The Calendar | The linked activity shows the new status. | Within a minute. |

Subscribers are emailed only if **Send to News On Demand subscribers** is ticked. If nobody follows the release's ministries, sectors, themes or tags, it publishes normally and no subscriber email is sent.

A reporter on a media list you ticked who also gets As-it-happens emails gets one email, the media version. If they chose the daily digest, the release is also in their digest.

Subscriber emails show the release's **Summary** from **Page details**, not its subheadline. Replies to a release email go to the news office's reply address.

Advisories are never sent to subscribers or put on the public site. They go only to their media lists, and once sent they can't be taken back.

## When you correct a published release

Saving any change republishes it at once, with no box asking first. The status shows **Republishing...**, then **Published**.

- The public page shows the change within a couple of minutes.
- Nobody is emailed again. People already emailed keep the first version; the 5 pm digest carries the corrected one.
- Media lists can't be changed on a release or factsheet once it has gone out.

## When you unpublish

- The release's page and its files come off the public site, and it leaves the lists on other pages.
- It leaves any Top or Feature spot it held.
- Emails not yet sent are stopped, and it stays out of later digests. Emails already sent can't be recalled.
- It goes back to **Drafts** as approved, with the same numbers and web address. Its status shows **Planned**, because it keeps its old publish time as a plan.

## When you publish it again after an unpublish

The release goes back on the public site at the same address. People who already got it aren't emailed again. The **Publish now** box says "Subscribers and media lists already received this when it was first published; they won't be emailed again."

If you unpublished it before all its emails had gone out, As-it-happens subscribers it hadn't reached are emailed when you publish it again. Daily-digest subscribers get it only if you republish before 5 pm on the day it first went out.

## Embargoes

There is no separate embargo setting. **Schedule** is the embargo: until the scheduled time, the public page, the files and the emails don't exist outside the Hub. Anyone with Hub access can read the release before then.
