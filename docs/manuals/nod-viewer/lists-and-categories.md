**Who this is for:** Subscribers viewers (NoD.Viewer).

**Lists and categories** shows every list people can subscribe to, how many active subscribers each one has, and whether it is offered. Subscribers administrators decide what is offered and in what order.

At the top, **All news** shows how many active subscribers chose all news. Under each category, such as **Ministries** or **Tags**, each list shows **Active subscribers** and **Offered**:

| Offered | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Yes** | People can choose it on the public site. |
| **No** | A Subscribers administrator stopped offering it. |
| **No (retired)** | Core administrators retired it, such as a ministry that no longer exists. Its name shows "(retired)". |
| **No (category not offered)** | A Subscribers administrator stopped offering its whole category. The category says "Not offered to subscribers." |
| **No (media list)** | A media list. People never choose media lists themselves. See [Media lists](manual:nod-viewer-media-lists). |

A subscriber who already has a list that is no longer offered keeps it, and still gets its releases.

Under each category the screen says who sets its names, such as "Names are set by Core administrators."
