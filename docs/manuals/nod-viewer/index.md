**Who this is for:** Subscribers viewers (NoD.Viewer).

You can look up the people who get news release email from News On Demand: their records, the media lists and the reports. You can't change anything, and you can't download email addresses. If something needs changing, ask a Subscribers editor.

Subscriber records are personal information. Look up addresses only when your work needs them.

## What you can do

| Where | What you do there | Help page |
|---|---|---|
| **Find subscribers** | Find someone by their address, and open their record and history. | [Find a subscriber](manual:nod-viewer-find-subscribers) |
| **Media lists** | See who is on each media list, who left it, and who needs attention. | [Media lists](manual:nod-viewer-media-lists) |
| **Lists and categories** | See which lists people can choose, and how many active subscribers each has. | [Lists and categories](manual:nod-viewer-lists-and-categories) |
| **Reports** | See who receives what, who left, and what was sent. Download the counts. | [Reports](manual:nod-viewer-reports) |

To answer "why isn't this person getting our email?", see [Who gets which email](manual:nod-viewer-who-gets-which-email).

## What you can't do

- Subscribers editors add subscribers, change their lists, timing and address, and deactivate or delete them. They also add and remove media list members. Their manual is the Subscribers editor manual.
- Subscribers administrators decide which lists are offered, and pause and resume email on **Operations**. Their manual is the Subscribers administrator manual.

If you open **Add a subscriber** or **Operations** by its address, you see "You don’t have permission to add subscribers." or "You don’t have permission to use Operations."

## What you see in the side menu

Under **Work** you see **Subscribers**. Its tabs are **Find subscribers**, **Lists and categories**, **Media lists** and **Reports**.

You may also see **Releases** or **Calendar** if you have a role there. Those have their own manuals.

## Keyboard shortcuts and Help

On **Find subscribers**, ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K on Windows goes to **Email contains**.

**Help**, at the top of every screen, opens the page of this manual for the screen you are on. The Help page also lists the keyboard shortcuts. Shortcuts that save, such as ⌘S or Ctrl+S, do nothing for you on the **Subscribers** screens.

## Signing in

Choose **Sign in with Microsoft** and use your government Microsoft account. You need a Hub account first: if you see "Your account hasn't been set up. Contact an administrator.", ask a Core administrator to add you. Signing out of the Hub doesn't sign you out of Microsoft.
