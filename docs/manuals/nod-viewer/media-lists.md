**Who this is for:** Subscribers viewers (NoD.Viewer).

**Media lists** holds the reporters on each media list. Release editors pick these lists on a release, and every member gets the full-text media version.

The table shows each list's **Members**, how many **Need attention**, and its **Status**. **Members** counts only the members who get the list's email. Disabled members get nothing, so they aren't counted; the cell then adds, for example, "(and 2 Disabled, who get nothing)". A **Retired** list can't be put on a release, and nothing is sent to it.

## See who is on a list

1. Choose a list's name. The list opens.
2. Read the members. Each shows their **Email**, their **Status**, their **Source** (**Media Hub**, **Added by hand** or **Subscriber**) and any problem under **Needs attention**. A line above the members says how many get this list's email. A member whose **Status** says "Disabled: gets nothing" is on the list but is sent nothing until an editor activates them on their record.
3. Choose an address to open that subscriber's record.

**All media lists** takes you back.

## Needs attention

| Reason | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Bouncing** | Their last 10 emails in 15 days all bounced. They are still on the list and still sent email. |
| **Media Hub email removed** | The address they use was removed from their Media Hub contact. |
| **Media Hub email belongs to another subscriber** | Media Hub moved them to an address another record already has. |
| **Media Hub email isn't valid** | Media Hub has an address that isn't a real email address. |
| **Media Hub email opted out of a media list this member is on** | Media Hub moved them to an address that unsubscribed from one of their lists, so they were kept at their old address. |

Subscribers editors fix these. Tell one, or Media Relations for a bouncing reporter.

## Left this list

This section lists people who left the list, with when they left (**Left**) and whether they are **Back on the list**. The screen explains "They unsubscribed, or left it in the old system." Someone removed by staff in the Hub isn't listed. If more than 200 have left, you see "Showing the most recent 200."

## Media Hub sync

Every night, News On Demand picks up changed Media Hub contacts. **Media Hub sync**, at the bottom of **Media lists**, shows when it last ran and what it changed, such as "60 changed contacts: 0 updated, 0 flagged, 0 removed, 0 skipped." Subscribers editors can run it sooner.

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Couldn't load media lists." or "Couldn't load this media list." | Reload the page. |
| "There's no media list with that key." | The address in your browser is wrong. Go back to **Media lists**. |
