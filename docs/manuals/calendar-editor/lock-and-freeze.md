**Who this is for:** Calendar editors (Calendar.Editor).

Two things can open an activity read-only even though you may change it: someone else editing it, and the daily change freeze.

## When someone else is editing

Only one person edits an activity at a time. The first person to change a field holds the edit lock. Anyone else who opens the activity sees, for example, "Pat Example is editing this activity (since 2:15 PM). It opens for editing when they save or cancel, or after 15 minutes without input."

The page stays read-only. It opens for you by itself, without a reload, once they save, cancel or leave it alone for 15 minutes.

## When your lock lapses

If you leave the page alone for 15 minutes, your lock lapses and you see "Your edit lock lapsed. Save will work only if no one else has taken it or changed the activity." Your changes stay on the page. Choose **Save**: it works if nobody else changed the activity. If someone did, you are asked to reload.

## When it is open in another tab

"You're editing this activity in another tab." means you are editing it in another tab or window. Choose **Continue here** to edit it in this one instead.

## The daily change freeze

Every day from 4pm to 5pm BC time, only HQ can make changes. The list and each activity show "You cannot make content changes between 4pm-5pm. Contact the Corp Cal Manager to have emerging or urgent updates made for you during this time."

During the freeze, unless you are in an HQ organization:

- **New activity** is hidden.
- Activities open read-only, with a **Change freeze** warning.
- **Clone** isn't offered, and files can't be added or removed.
- A save you started before 4pm and send after 4pm is refused with the same message. Your changes stay on the page. Save again at 5pm.

These still work during the freeze: the list, filters, **My Queries**, the star, **Updates**, **View changes**, the Excel export and the reports.

For an urgent change during the freeze, contact the Corp Cal Manager at GCPE HQ.

If you are an HQ editor, the freeze doesn't apply to you. You see the times on the list, but you can keep working.
