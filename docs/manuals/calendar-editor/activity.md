**Who this is for:** Calendar editors (Calendar.Editor).

You add activities for your ministries, and change the activities your ministries lead. In an HQ organization you can add and change any ministry's activities you can see. What each field is for is on [Activity fields](manual:calendar-editor-fields).

## Add an activity

1. On **Calendar**, choose **New activity**.
2. Fill in the fields. The times start at 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. If you have only one ministry, it is already the **Lead Ministry**.
3. Choose **Save**. You see, for example, "Created HLTH-21." The activity stays open, so you can add files.

**New activity** is hidden during the daily change freeze, unless you are in an HQ organization.

## Change an activity

1. Open the activity: choose its title in the list or the calendar, or its number on **Updates**.
2. Make your changes. As soon as you change something, the activity is yours to edit: anyone else who opens it sees it read-only until you finish.
3. Choose **Save**. You are back where you came from and see, for example, "Saved HLTH-21."

**Cancel** goes back without saving. If you have unsaved changes, the Hub asks "You have unsaved changes to this activity. Leave anyway and discard them?" Choose **Stay** or **Leave**.

## If Save finds a problem

A box at the top, "Fix these to save", lists each problem, such as "Enter a title" or "Choose a comm contact". Choose one to go to its field. Nothing is saved until they are fixed.

A start or end date in the past is only a warning: "The start date is in the past." or "The end date is in the past." The activity still saves.

## What happens when you save

- A new activity is **New**. Saving a change to most fields makes it **Changed**, until HQ reviews it.
- HQ sees which fields changed, marked "Changed: needs review".
- Everyone who can see the activity sees your change straight away, in the list, on **Updates** and in reports.
- The change reaches **Releases** within a minute. A release already linked to the activity doesn't change. See [Releases and the Forecast](manual:calendar-editor-releases).
- Every change is kept in **View changes**.
- Nobody is emailed. Tell people yourself if they need to know.

## Activities you can only view

"Your ministry is shared on this activity: you can view it but not change it." means another ministry leads it. The fields are read-only, each checklist shows only what is ticked (or "None"), and the button is **Back**.

An activity also opens read-only while someone else is editing it, or during the daily change freeze. See [The edit lock and the daily change freeze](manual:calendar-editor-lock-and-freeze).

## Clone an activity

**Clone** makes a new activity from this one, for the same lead ministry and comm contact.

1. Open the activity.
2. Choose **Clone**. The new activity opens, and you see, for example, "Cloned HLTH-21 as HLTH-22."
3. Check it, change what differs, and choose **Save**.

The clone is saved as soon as you choose **Clone**, and starts as **New**. It copies the activity's fields except these:

- the release date and time are emptied;
- **Internal notes** is emptied;
- **News Subscribe** is emptied;
- of the **HQ Tags**, only "30-60-90" is kept;
- files and linked releases stay with the original.

If you have unsaved changes, **Clone** waits: "Save or cancel your changes to review or clone." **Clone** isn't offered during the daily change freeze, unless you are in an HQ organization.

## Add files

The **Records** section appears only when it is turned on for your Calendar. When it shows:

1. Save a new activity first. Until then it says "Save the activity first to add files."
2. Under **Records**, choose **Add files** and pick one or more files.
3. Each file is added straight away, on its own. You see, for example, "Added 2 files."

A file can be a PDF, a PNG, JPEG or GIF image, a Word, Excel or PowerPoint file, an Outlook message, RTF, text or CSV. Each can be up to 25 MB, and an activity holds up to 50. A file with the same name replaces the old one.

To remove a file, choose **Remove** beside it, then **Remove** again in the "The file is deleted from this activity." box.

Adding or removing a file doesn't touch your unsaved changes to the fields. Like saving, it isn't possible while someone else is editing the activity, or during the daily change freeze unless you are in an HQ organization. A file opens only for people who can see its activity.

## View changes

**View changes** lists every change to the activity, newest first. Each entry says when, who and what they did, such as "created it", "changed it", "created it as a clone", "reviewed it", "transferred it" or "cleared its LA status". A table shows each field before and after. Changes brought over from the old Calendar are marked "from legacy log".

Choose **Back to the activity** to return.

## Delete an activity

You can't delete activities, so **Delete** isn't offered. Ask a Calendar administrator of the lead ministry, or an HQ administrator.

## If your session runs out

If you are signed out while you have unsaved changes, sign in again and open the same activity. It says "Your unsaved changes were restored." Signing out yourself discards them.

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "It doesn't exist, or you can't see it." (under "Activity not found") | Check the number. The activity may be led by a ministry you're not in, or it was deleted. |
| "Someone else changed this activity — reload to see their changes" | Someone saved it after you opened it. Choose **Reload**. If you have unsaved changes, it asks "Reload and lose your changes? The activity as it is now replaces them." Choose **Reload**, then make your change again, or **Keep editing**. |
| "This activity is no longer available." | It was deleted, or moved out of your ministries, after you opened it. |
| "This activity is deleted" | Someone deleted it while you were editing. Your changes stay on the page but can't be saved. |
| "You can only choose one of your ministries" | Choose one of your own ministries as the **Lead Ministry**. |
| "Choose a comm contact of the lead ministry" | The comm contact belongs to another ministry. Choose another. |
| "That comm contact is no longer active" or "That person's account is inactive" | Choose another comm contact. An activity that already has them still saves, but can't be cloned. |
| "That category is no longer in use" | Choose another category. |
| "Couldn't save. Your changes are still here; try again." | Something went wrong on the way. Choose **Save** again. |
