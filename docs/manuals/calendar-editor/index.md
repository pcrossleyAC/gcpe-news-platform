**Who this is for:** Calendar editors (Calendar.Editor).

You keep your ministry's activities in the Corporate Calendar up to date. The Corporate Calendar is the government-wide plan of announcements and events. You add and change activities, follow what changed on **Updates**, and make reports.

## Ministry or HQ

Whether one of your ministries is an HQ organization, such as GCPE Headquarters, decides what you can do.

- **In a ministry:** you see your ministries' activities and the activities shared with them. You add and change your ministries' activities.
- **In an HQ organization:** you see every ministry's activities, except other ministries' ones marked **Not for Look Ahead**. You add and change any ministry's activities you can see, and the daily change freeze doesn't stop you. See [If you are an HQ editor](manual:calendar-editor-hq).

## What you can do that read-only users can't

Calendar read-only users see the list, **Updates**, activities and reports, but can't change anything. These are yours:

| Where | What you do there | Help page |
|---|---|---|
| **New activity**, above the list | Add an activity. | [Add or change an activity](manual:calendar-editor-activity) |
| An activity | Change it and choose **Save**. | [Add or change an activity](manual:calendar-editor-activity) |
| **Clone**, on an activity | Make a new activity from it. | [Add or change an activity](manual:calendar-editor-activity) |
| **Records**, on an activity | Add and remove files, when your Calendar has this section. | [Add or change an activity](manual:calendar-editor-activity) |
| **LA status**, above the list | HQ editors only: clear the Look Ahead's NEW and CHANGED flags. | [If you are an HQ editor](manual:calendar-editor-hq) |

To see what a read-only user can do, read the [Calendar read-only manual](manual:calendar-readonly).

## What you can't do

Ask a Calendar administrator to:

- delete an activity;
- give or change someone's Calendar access;
- change the choices offered on activities, such as categories, cities and HQ tags;
- move activities from one comm contact to another.

Reviewing activities, corporate queries and the **Look Ahead filter** are for HQ advanced users. Their manual is the Calendar advanced user manual. Calendar administrators have the Calendar administrator manual.

## Who sees an activity

- Its lead ministry, and every ministry ticked in **Shared With**, see it.
- HQ organizations see every ministry's activities.
- An activity marked **Not for Look Ahead** is seen only by its lead and shared ministries, and by HQ advanced users and HQ administrators. HQ editors don't see other ministries' ones.
- Only the lead ministry and HQ can change an activity. A ministry it is only shared with can open it but not change it.
- A deleted activity leaves the Calendar for everyone except HQ administrators.

## What you see in the side menu

Under **Work** you see **Calendar**. Its tabs are **Calendar** and **Updates**.

You may also see **Releases** or **Subscribers** if you have a role there. Those have their own manuals.

## When the list is empty

"You aren't in any ministry yet, so there are no activities to show you. Ask a Calendar administrator." means your Calendar access has no ministry. A Calendar administrator adds it.

"You don't have Calendar access. Ask a Calendar administrator." means your Calendar access was removed. You stay signed in to the Hub.

## Keyboard shortcuts

On a Mac use Command (⌘); on Windows use Ctrl.

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| ⌘K or Ctrl+K | On the activity list, opens **Filters** and goes to **Search for**. |
| ⌘S or Ctrl+S | On an activity, saves it, the same as **Save**. |
| ⌘Enter or Ctrl+Enter | On a new activity, saves it. |

## Signing in

Choose **Sign in with Microsoft** and use your government Microsoft account. You need a Hub account first: if you see "Your account hasn't been set up. Contact an administrator.", ask a Core administrator to add you. Signing out of the Hub doesn't sign you out of Microsoft.
