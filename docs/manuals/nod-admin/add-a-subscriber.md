**Who this is for:** Subscribers administrators (NoD.Admin).

**Add a subscriber** signs someone up for news release email on their behalf. Add only people who have asked to receive it.

## Add someone

1. Enter their address in **Email** and again in **Confirm email**.
2. Under **Lists**, tick **All news**, or tick the lists they want.
3. Tick **As it happens**, **Daily digest**, or both. **As it happens** starts ticked.
4. Choose **Add subscriber**. Their new record opens.

## What happens next

- They are **Active** straight away. No confirmation email is sent, and they are not told they were added.
- They get the next release that matches their lists. See [Who gets which email](manual:who-gets-which-email).
- Their record shows **Source** "Added by staff", and **History** shows "Added by staff" with your name.

To put a reporter on a media list, use **Media lists** instead. See [Media lists](manual:subscriber-media-lists).

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "The two email addresses don't match." | Type the same address in both boxes. |
| "Choose at least one list, or all news." | Tick a list or **All news**. |
| "Choose As It Happens, Daily Digest, or both." | Tick at least one timing. |
| "That address already has a subscriber record." | Choose **Open their record**. If they are **Disabled**, activate them there. If they unsubscribed or were deleted, only they can sign up again, on the public site. |
| "Couldn't load the list of lists. “All news” still works." | The lists didn't load. Reload the page, or add them to **All news**. |
