**Who this is for:** Subscribers administrators (NoD.Admin).

Only **Active** subscribers get news email. **Pending**, **Disabled** and **Unsubscribed or deleted** subscribers get none.

## News releases

Each subscriber chooses lists and a timing. Lists are ministries, sectors, themes and tags. **All news** covers every release that names a ministry.

| Timing | What they get |
|---|---|
| **As it happens** | One email for each matching release, soon after it is published. |
| **Daily digest** | One email at 5:00 pm BC time listing the matching releases since the last digest. Media advisories are left out. No matching releases means no digest. |
| **As it happens and daily digest** | Both. |
| **None (media lists only)** | No news release email. This person is here only for media lists. |

A correction to a release never emails anyone again. If a release is unpublished, anything not yet sent is stopped, and it stays out of later digests.

## Emergency alerts

Alerts posted by Emergency Info BC are checked every 5 minutes. A new alert goes at once to everyone active on the **Emergency Info BC Alerts** list, whatever timing they chose. An alert edited later is not sent again.

## Media lists

Release editors choose media lists for a release. Each member of those lists gets the full-text media version. A reporter who is also an **As it happens** subscriber for that release gets one email, the media version, not two.

## Emails about the subscription itself

People who sign up on the public site get an email asking them to confirm. They have no record here until they do. They also get an email when they ask to change their preferences or address. These emails still go out while sending is paused.

Nothing you do on the **Subscribers** screens emails the subscriber. Adding, changing, deactivating and deleting are all silent. Tell the person yourself if they need to know.
