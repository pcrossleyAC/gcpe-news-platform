**Who this is for:** Subscribers administrators (NoD.Admin).

You run News On Demand, the service that emails news releases to the public and to reporters. You decide which lists people can subscribe to, you can stop and start all outgoing email, and you watch bounces.

## What only you can do

Subscribers editors and Subscribers viewers can open the same screens, but only you can change these.

| Screen | What it controls | Who notices | Help page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Subscribers** > **Lists and categories** | Which lists the public can choose, and their order. | People signing up on the public site, and staff adding subscribers. Existing subscribers keep their lists. | [Lists and categories](manual:lists-and-categories) |
| **Subscribers** > **Operations** | Pausing News On Demand sending or all email, the daily bounce summary, which soft bounces count, and the retention purge. | Every subscriber and reporter. A pause holds their email until you resume. | [Operations](manual:subscriber-operations) |

Only you see the **Operations** tab.

## What you share with Subscribers editors

Subscribers editors can do these as well. Subscribers viewers can only look.

| Screen | What you do there | Help page |
|---|---|---|
| **Find subscribers** | Search, change preferences and email addresses, deactivate, delete. | [Find and change subscribers](manual:find-and-change-subscribers) |
| **Add a subscriber** | Sign someone up without a confirmation email. | [Add a subscriber](manual:add-a-subscriber) |
| **Media lists** | Add and remove reporters, and fix flagged members. | [Media lists](manual:subscriber-media-lists) |
| **Reports** | Counts, unsubscribes and sends, with CSV downloads. | [Reports](manual:subscriber-reports) |

The [Subscribers editor manual](manual:nod-editor) and the [Subscribers viewer manual](manual:nod-viewer) are written for those roles.

## Before you start

- [Who gets which email](manual:who-gets-which-email) explains what each subscriber receives and when.
- [Bounces](manual:bounces) explains how bounced email disables a subscriber, and what the daily bounce summary tells you.

Subscriber records are personal information. Search for, download and keep addresses only when your work needs them.

## What you see in the side menu

Under **Work** you see **Subscribers**. Its tabs are **Find subscribers**, **Add a subscriber**, **Lists and categories**, **Media lists**, **Reports** and **Operations**.

Media list names are set by Core administrators on their **Media list names** screen. Ask a Core administrator to add, rename or retire a media list.
