**Who this is for:** Calendar administrators (Calendar.Administrator) and Calendar system administrators (Calendar.SysAdmin).

You run the Corporate Calendar: who can use it, the choices offered on activities, and which comm contact looks after each activity. If you belong to an HQ organization, you also review what the ministries enter.

## Which administrator you are

Your role, and whether one of your ministries is an HQ organization such as GCPE Headquarters, decide what you can do.

- **Administrator**: you manage Calendar users and their access. You transfer and delete activities of your own ministries.
- **HQ Administrator**: an Administrator or system administrator whose ministries include an HQ organization. You see every ministry's activities, deleted ones included. You review activities, and you can transfer and delete activities of any ministry.
- **System Administrator**: everything an Administrator can do, plus every lookup, the **Undelivered events** tab, and giving the **System Administrator** role. You can also change the access of HQ staff.

## What only you can do

Calendar advanced users, editors and read-only users can't do any of this.

## What you share with other Calendar users

Everyone with Calendar access uses the list, filters, **My Queries**, **Updates**, **Excel export** and the **Look Ahead**, **30/60/90** and **Planning** reports. Editors and above create, change and clone their ministries' activities. HQ staff at Advanced and above also have **Corporate Queries**, the **Look Ahead filter** and **Review**.

The [Calendar advanced user manual](manual:calendar-advanced), the [Calendar editor manual](manual:calendar-editor) and the [Calendar read-only manual](manual:calendar-readonly) cover that work.

## Where your changes show up

- Role, ministry and account changes reach the Calendar within a minute.
- Activity changes reach **Releases** within a minute. Release editors who also have Calendar access see confirmed activities with an "Approved" category on the **Forecast** tab, and can plan a release from them. Confidential and deleted activities never appear there.
- Nothing in this manual emails anyone.

## What you see in the side menu

Under **Work** you see **Calendar**. Under **Administration** you see **Calendar access**.

The Calendar's tabs are **Calendar**, **Updates**, **Lookups**, **Users** and **Transfer**. System administrators also see **Undelivered events**.
