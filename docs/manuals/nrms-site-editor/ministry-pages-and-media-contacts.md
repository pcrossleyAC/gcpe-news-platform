**Who this is for:** Site editors (NRMS.SiteEditor).

Two settings screens set the wording that every ministry shares. A single ministry's own details, such as its minister and contacts, are on [Organizations](manual:site-editor-organizations). Both screens change the public site only when you publish. See [How site settings are published](manual:site-settings).

## Ministry page headings

**Website** > **Site settings** > **Ministry page headings** sets the seven headings on every ministry's public page: **More from this Ministry heading**, **Ministry website heading**, **Ministry photos heading**, **Ministry videos heading**, **Featured topics heading**, **Featured services heading** and **Media contacts heading**.

1. Change the heading.
2. Choose **Publish**.

Each heading is required: plain text, up to 60 characters. **Reset to defaults** puts the standard headings back in the boxes. You see "Headings reset to the defaults. Save or publish to keep them."

The ministry pages change within a few minutes.

## Media contacts page

**Website** > **Site settings** > **Media contacts page** sets the public page that lists media contacts.

- **Page heading** (required), **Contact line heading** and **List heading**.
- **Introduction**: formatted text with bold, italic, lists and links. Include the 24-hour media line number here.
- **Other media contacts**: contacts that aren't a ministry's, such as a central media line. Each ministry's own contacts come from its **Organizations** form.

To add another contact:

1. Choose **Add contact**.
2. Fill in **Group** and **Name** (both required), and if you like **Group link**, **Subgroup**, **Phone**, **Cell** and **Email**.
3. Under **Placement**, choose **Above the ministries** or **Below the ministries**.
4. Use **Move contact 1 up**, **Move contact 1 down** or **Remove contact 1** to arrange the list. You can have up to 30.
5. Choose **Publish**.

A contact row left completely blank is dropped when you save.

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Enter a heading." | A ministry page heading is empty. |
| "Use plain text, 60 characters or fewer." | Shorten the ministry page heading, and remove any < or > characters. |
| "Enter text for this field." | A required box, such as **Page heading**, **Group** or **Name**, is empty. |
| "Enter an email address like name@gov.bc.ca." | Fix the contact's **Email**. |
| "Must start with https:// or / (a page on this site)." | Fix the contact's **Group link**. |
| "Keep the list to 30 or fewer." | Remove a contact. |
