**Who this is for:** Site editors (NRMS.SiteEditor).

**Resource links** is the list of links shown on the public home page under the **Resources** heading, in the order you set.

## Change the links

1. To change a link, edit its **Link 1 text** or **Link 1 URL** (and so on for each link).
2. To add one, choose **Add link** and fill in the new boxes at the end.
3. To move one, choose **Move link 1 up** or **Move link 1 down**, or drag it by its handle.
4. To remove one, choose **Remove link 1**. It goes at once, with no question, but nothing is saved yet.
5. Choose **Save links**. This saves the whole list as it stands. You see "Saved at 2:30 PM. Live now: the public home page shows the resource links within a few minutes." (with the time).

A link's address must start with http:// or https://, or with / for a page on the public site, such as /files/media-guide.pdf. For a file, you can also paste the address from its **Copy public address** button on [Files](manual:website-files). An address copied from the Hub itself shows "This address points at the Hub, which the public can't open. On Website → Files, use Copy public address and paste that instead."

If you change your mind before saving, reload the page. The list goes back to what is saved.

## What happens next

- The home page shows the new list within a few minutes.
- **Website log** lists "Saved 3 resource links" (with the number), with your name.

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "{link text}" needs an http(s) URL or one starting with "/". | That link's address is missing or has the wrong form. |
| "{link text}" needs text of 255 characters or fewer. | Shorten the link's words. |
| "Someone else changed this — reload to see their changes." | Another site editor saved the list while you had the page open. Choose **Reload**. Your unsaved changes are lost. |
