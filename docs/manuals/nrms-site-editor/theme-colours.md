**Who this is for:** Site editors (NRMS.SiteEditor).

**Theme colours** sets the public site's colours: **Header and buttons**, **Text on the header and buttons**, **Accent**, **Body text**, **Secondary text**, **Panels**, **Page background**, **Borders**, **Information banner**, **Emergency banner** and **Section bar colour (Connect page)**. Open it from **Website** > **Site settings** > **Theme**.

## Change a colour

1. Type the colour's code, such as #013366, in its box. Or choose the colour square beside it and pick a colour.
2. Check the badge beside the colour. "Readable 7.7:1" means text on it is easy to read. "Too faint 2.3:1" means it isn't.
3. Check the **Preview**. It shows the colours you are editing, not the live ones.
4. Choose **Publish**. See [How site settings are published](manual:site-settings).

A colour that is too faint can be saved in your draft, but it can't be published. Pick a darker shade until every badge says Readable.

**Reset to defaults** puts the standard colours back in the boxes. You see "Colours reset to the defaults. Save or publish to keep them." Nothing changes on the site until you publish.

## What visitors see

New colours show within a few minutes of publishing.

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Use a colour like #013366." | The code must be # and six letters or digits. |
| "Too faint to read behind white text (2.3:1, needs 4.5:1). Pick a darker shade." | Text on or in this colour would be hard to read. The message says where: on the page background, on panels, on the header, as link text or behind white text. |
