**Who this is for:** Site editors (NRMS.SiteEditor).

**Page text** sets the wording on the public pages that aren't news releases. Open it from **Website** > **Site settings** > **Page text**. It changes the public site only when you publish. See [How site settings are published](manual:site-settings).

Only two boxes are required: the page not found **Heading** and the privacy page **Title**. Leave any other box empty to hide that wording.

| Part | Boxes | Where visitors see it |
|---|---|---|
| **Page not found** | **Heading**, **Text** | When they open an address that doesn't exist. |
| **Privacy page** | **Label**, **Title**, **Statement** | The privacy page. For a heading inside the statement, put bold text on a line of its own. |
| **Live webcast page** | **Heading**, **Before play**, **When not live** | The webcast page. In **Before play**, {host} is replaced by the stream's host name. |
| **Subscribe page** | **Notice heading**, **Notice of Collection**, **Help line** | The page where people subscribe to news by email. |
| **Media assets licence** | **Text**, **Link text**, **Link URL** | After the media assets list on each release, as the text, then the link, then a period. The period is added for you. |
| **Search: older archives** | A list of links | The search page, linking to older news archives. |

**Notice of Collection** is legal wording. Changes may need review by your privacy office before you publish.

The **Statement**, **Before play**, **When not live**, **Notice of Collection** and **Help line** boxes take formatted text: bold, italic, bulleted and numbered lists, and links.

## Change the wording

1. Change the box.
2. Choose **Publish**.

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Enter text for this field." | The page not found **Heading** or the privacy page **Title** is empty. |
| "Keep it to 300 characters or fewer." | Shorten the wording. The limit depends on the box. |
| "Use plain text." | Remove < and > from a plain text box. |
| "Enter the link text." | The media assets licence, or an archive link, has a web address but no words. |
| "Must start with https:// or / (a page on this site)." | A link's web address is missing, starts with http://, or has a space in it. |
| "Keep the list to 10 or fewer." | There are more than 10 older archive links. |
