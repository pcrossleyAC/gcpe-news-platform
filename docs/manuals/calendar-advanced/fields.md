**Who this is for:** Calendar advanced users (Calendar.Advanced).

An activity's fields are in sections, in this order: **Overview**, **Ministry**, **Planning**, **Schedule**, **Release**, **Event** and, when turned on, **Records**. In an HQ organization you also see **Look Ahead**, after **Event**. A "?" beside a field explains it.

## What you must fill in

Fields marked "(required)" must be filled in before you can save:

- **Category**, **Title**, **Lead Ministry** and **Comm Contact**;
- **Start date** and **End date**, and both times unless you tick **All Day**;
- **Summary**, **Significance** and **Scheduling considerations**. In an HQ organization these three are optional.

A category that is a release, such as **Approved Release**, also needs **Origin**, **Distribution** and at least one **Comm Materials**. The message says, for example, "Choose the origin: this category is a release".

## Overview

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Category** | The kind of activity. **HQ Placeholder** is offered only to HQ. |
| **Confidential (not for Look Ahead)** | Tick it to make the activity confidential. The old Calendar called it **Not for Look Ahead**. Only its lead and shared ministries, HQ advanced users and HQ Administrators then see it, and it isn't offered on the releases **Forecast**. A new activity isn't given a Look Ahead section, but ticking it doesn't take away a section the activity already has. |
| **Title** | Up to 100 characters. |
| **Summary** | Up to 700 characters. |
| **Issue** | Tick it if the activity is an issue. |
| **Significance** | Why it matters. Up to 500 characters. |
| **Lead Organization** | The organization leading it, when that isn't the lead ministry, such as a partner. Up to 80 characters. |
| **HQ Initiatives & Leads** | Tick the initiatives it supports. |
| **HQ Tags** | Type a tag and choose **Add tag**, or press Enter. Existing tags are suggested as you type. A new tag is created when you save. Up to 20. Choose × beside a tag to remove it. |

## Ministry

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Lead Ministry** | One of your ministries. In an HQ organization, any active ministry. Some ministries can't lead activities and aren't offered. Changing it empties **Comm Contact** if the contact belongs to another ministry. |
| **Cross-Government** | Tick it for a cross-government activity. It doesn't change who sees the activity. |
| **Shared With** | Tick the other ministries that should see it. They can open it but not change it. |

## Planning

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Comm Contact** | The communications person responsible. Only the active comm contacts of the **Lead Ministry**, chosen above, are offered. |
| **Key activity** | Tick it for a key activity. |
| **Strategy** | Up to 500 characters. |
| **Comm Materials** | The products planned, such as a news release. |
| **Internal notes** | Up to 4,000 characters. |

## Schedule

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **All Day** | Tick it for an all-day activity. It hides the times below it. |
| **Dates Confirmed** | Tick it once the date is firm. Only confirmed activities are offered on the releases **Forecast**. |
| **Start date**, **Start time** | Times go in 5-minute steps. |
| **End date**, **End time** | The end can't be before the start. |
| **Potential Dates** | A general timeline, like "late June". No numbers, TBC or TBD. Up to 50 characters. |
| **Scheduling considerations** | Anything that affects the timing. Up to 500 characters. |

## Release

Some categories, such as **TV / Radio** and **Awareness Day / Week / Month**, have no release. For those this section is hidden, and choosing one empties the release date and time.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Release date**, **Release time** | When the news release goes out. Fill in both, or leave both empty: **Release time** starts at "Choose a time". It can't be after the end date. |
| **Origin**, **Distribution** | Where the release comes from, and how widely it goes. |
| **Translations Required** | The languages needed. |
| **Sectors**, **Themes**, **News Subscribe** | The release's topics. A release editor can copy them into a release. |
| **BC Gov News** | The news releases linked to this activity. See [Releases and the Forecast](manual:calendar-advanced-releases). |

## Event

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Premier Requested** | Whether the Premier was asked to take part, such as **Yes**, **No** or **Invited**. |
| **Representative** | Who represents government at the activity. |
| **At BC Legislature** | Tick it if it takes place there. |
| **City**, **Venue** | Where it takes place. When you choose the city for places not on the list, **Other City** appears: type the city there. Venue and Other City take up to 55 characters. |
| **Event Planner**, **Digital** | Who plans the event, and the videographer. |

## Look Ahead

Only HQ sees this section. Other users' saves leave it as it is.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Executive Summary** | Shown in HQ's Look Ahead report. Up to 2,000 characters. |
| **LA Status** | **None**, **New** or **Changed**. HQ's Look Ahead flags the activity NEW or CHANGED. |
| **LA Section** | Where the activity goes in the Look Ahead. It is worked out from fields above it: **Category**, **Issue**, **Confidential (not for Look Ahead)**, **Lead Ministry**, **Comm Materials**, **Dates Confirmed** and the start and end dates. Its hint names them. Choose another section to override it; it then says "Override" and offers **Use the inferred section**. Awareness dates and activities led by the ministry shown as **CITENG** keep a fixed section, which can't be overridden. |
| **Long Term Outlook** | Tick it to list the activity in the Look Ahead's Long Term Outlook. |

An activity marked **Confidential (not for Look Ahead)** whose section is overridden needs an **Executive Summary**.

For **Records**, see [Add or change an activity](manual:calendar-advanced-activity).

## Choices no longer in use

A choice that has been retired shows "(no longer in use)" when an activity already has it. The activity keeps it. You can't choose it for another activity.

## Long text from the old Calendar

Some activities brought over from the old Calendar have longer text than the limits allow. They still save. The limits apply only to text you change.
