**Who this is for:** Calendar advanced users (Calendar.Advanced).

**Calendar** > **Updates** lists recent changes to the activities you can see, newest first. It opens on **Today's updates**. **Latest 5 updates** shows the five newest.

Each line says when, who, and what they did: "added activity", "changed activity", "reviewed activity", "deleted activity" or "added new clone". Then come the activity's number, its title and its dates as they are now. Point at the title to see the summary.

Choose the activity's number to open it. **Save** or **Cancel** there brings you back to the same updates.

## Find older updates

1. Fill in **From** and **To**. **From** starts at yesterday. Both days are included. Leave either one empty to leave that end open.
2. To narrow it, choose an **Update type**: **Changed**, **Added**, **Deleted**, **Reviewed** or **Cloned**.
3. To narrow it further, type in **Search for**: words in the activity's title or summary, or the name of who made the change.
4. Choose **Search**.

To see one activity's updates, type its number above 10,000, such as HLTH-20001, in **Search for**. Shorter numbers, and words like COVID-19, are searched as text.

At most 1,000 updates show at once. "Showing the newest 1,000. Narrow the dates or the search to see older updates." means there were more.

## What isn't here

- Changes from before the new Calendar started. Open the activity and choose **View changes**: older changes are marked "from legacy log".
- Transfers between comm contacts, and **Clear LA Status**. Those show only on the activity's **View changes**.
- Activities you can no longer see. When an activity is marked **Not for Look Ahead**, moved to another ministry or deleted, its updates leave the list for anyone who can't see it now.
- Changes to the **Look Ahead** fields, unless you see those fields. In an HQ organization you do.

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "From must be on or before To." | Fix the dates. |
| "No updates." | Nothing changed in that time, or nothing matches the search. |
| "Activity not found: it doesn't exist, or you can't see it." | Check the number. |
