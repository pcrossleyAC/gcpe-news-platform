**Who this is for:** Calendar advanced users (Calendar.Advanced).

You plan the Corporate Calendar: the government-wide schedule of announcements and events. You add and change activities, follow what changed on **Updates**, and make reports.

## Ministry or HQ

Whether one of your ministries is an HQ organization, such as GCPE Headquarters, decides what you can do.

- **In a ministry:** you see your ministries' activities and the activities shared with them. You add and change your ministries' activities. You can do exactly what a Calendar editor can.
- **In an HQ organization:** you see every ministry's activities, including those marked **Not for Look Ahead**. You add and change any ministry's activities, and the daily change freeze doesn't stop you. You also review activities and run corporate queries.

## What you can do that editors and read-only users can't

Calendar read-only users look at activities, **Updates** and reports, but can't add or change anything. Calendar editors add and change activities as you do.

In an HQ organization, Advanced gives you four things that HQ editors don't have:

| Where | What you do there | Help page |
|---|---|---|
| The activity list | See other ministries' activities marked **Not for Look Ahead**. | [The activity list](manual:calendar-advanced-list) |
| **Saved views** > **Corporate Queries** | List every ministry's upcoming activities by status. | [HQ review and corporate queries](manual:calendar-advanced-hq) |
| **Look Ahead filter**, above the list | Show only the activities for the Look Ahead, or only those not for it. | [HQ review and corporate queries](manual:calendar-advanced-hq) |
| **Review**, on an activity | Mark the activity reviewed. | [HQ review and corporate queries](manual:calendar-advanced-hq) |

In a ministry that isn't HQ, Advanced gives you nothing more than Editor.

To see what the others can do, read the [Calendar editor manual](manual:calendar-editor) and the [Calendar read-only manual](manual:calendar-readonly).

## What you can't do

Ask a Calendar administrator to:

- delete an activity;
- give or change someone's Calendar access;
- change the choices offered on activities, such as categories, cities and HQ tags;
- move activities from one comm contact to another.

HQ Administrators also review deletions, review many activities at once and make the **Exec Look Ahead** report. The Calendar administrator manual covers this work.

## Who sees an activity

- Its lead ministry, and every ministry ticked in **Shared With**, see it.
- HQ organizations see every ministry's activities.
- An activity marked **Not for Look Ahead** is seen only by its lead and shared ministries, and by HQ advanced users and HQ Administrators. HQ editors don't see other ministries' ones.
- Only the lead ministry and HQ can change an activity. A ministry it is only shared with can open it but not change it.
- A deleted activity leaves the Calendar for everyone except HQ Administrators.

## What you see in the side menu

Under **Work** you see **Calendar**. Its tabs are **Calendar** and **Updates**.

You may also see **Releases** or **Subscribers** if you have a role there. Those have their own manuals.

## When the list is empty

"You aren't in any ministry yet, so there are no activities to show you. Ask a Calendar administrator." means your Calendar access has no ministry. A Calendar administrator adds it.

"You don't have Calendar access. Ask a Calendar administrator." means your Calendar access was removed. You stay signed in to the Hub.

## Keyboard shortcuts

On a Mac use Command (⌘); on Windows use Ctrl.

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| ⌘K or Ctrl+K | On the activity list, opens **Filters** and goes to **Search for**. |
| ⌘S or Ctrl+S | On an activity, saves it, the same as **Save**. |
| ⌘Enter or Ctrl+Enter | On a new activity, saves it. |

## Signing in

Choose **Sign in with Microsoft** and use your government Microsoft account. You need a Hub account first: if you see "Your account hasn't been set up. Contact an administrator.", ask a Core administrator to add you. Signing out of the Hub doesn't sign you out of Microsoft.
