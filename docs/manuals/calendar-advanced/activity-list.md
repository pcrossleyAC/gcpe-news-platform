**Who this is for:** Calendar advanced users (Calendar.Advanced).

**Calendar** lists the activities you can see, from today on, earliest first. The first 30 show. More load as you scroll down, or when you choose **Show more** (the button names how many).

Two kinds of activity are left out until you ask for them:

- Awareness dates. Choose **Awareness Day / Week / Month** in **Category** to see them.
- Activities led by the consultations ministry, shown as **CITENG**. To see them, choose a ministry in **Lead Ministry**: in a ministry, choose your own (this also lists activities shared with it); in an HQ organization, choose **CITENG**.

Both kinds always show under **My Watchlist Only**.

## Read the list

- Choose an activity's title to open it. When you come back, its row is brought into view.
- The **Activity Id** column shows the ministry and number, such as HLTH-12. It also shows **Reviewed** when HQ has reviewed the activity, **Shared** when it is shared with other ministries, and **Release** when a news release is linked to it.
- Under the title you see when it was last updated, by whom, and when it was created.
- **Confidential (not for Look Ahead)** under **Categories** marks a confidential activity. See [Who sees an activity](manual:calendar-advanced-overview).
- Point at the date to see the scheduling considerations, at the title to see the significance, and at **Comm Materials** to see the origin and distribution.
- Choose a column heading to sort by it, except **Premier Requested**. Choose it again to reverse the order.
- **What the column names mean**, under the list, explains the short names.

## Filter the list

1. Open **Filters**. The line beside it shows the filter in use, or "No filters".
2. Fill in what you want. You can use **From**, **To**, **This day only**, **Search for**, **HQ Tags**, **Issue**, **Dates Confirmed**, **Status**, **Category**, **Lead Ministry**, **Comm Contact**, **Representative**, **HQ Initiatives & Leads**, **Premier Requested** and **Distribution**.
3. Under **Display**, choose **Show All**, **My Ministries' Activities Only**, **My Activities Only** or **My Watchlist Only**.
4. Choose **Apply filters**.

What some choices do:

- **Search for** takes an activity number, such as HLTH-12345, or words in the title, summary, city and other text. An activity number finds that activity whatever the other filters and dates say; the line beside **Filters** then says, for example, "Activity number: HLTH-12345 only". A plain number without the ministry counts as an activity number only above 10,000; a smaller one, such as a year, is searched as a word.
- **Lead Ministry** also finds activities shared with that ministry.
- **My Ministries' Activities Only** shows activities your ministries lead, not those only shared with them.
- **My Activities Only** shows activities where you are the comm contact.
- **My Watchlist Only** shows the activities you have starred.

**Reset** empties the filter. Choose **Apply filters** to run it.

The Hub keeps your **Display** choice for next time. To share a filtered list, copy the page address. If the link uses HQ tools you don't have, the list opens without them.

## Save a filter

Under **Saved views**, **My Queries** keeps filters you use often. Only you see them.

1. Set the filter and choose **Apply filters**.
2. Open **Saved views**.
3. Type a name in **Name for this query**.
4. Choose **Save this filter as a query**. You see, for example, "Saved the query “My week”."

A query saves the filter the list is running. While **Filters** has changes you haven't applied, **Save this filter as a query** waits and says "Apply filters first". **Display** isn't saved with a query.

To run a saved filter, choose its name. **Saved views** then shows, for example, "Running “My week”". Each query also has **Rename**, **Up**, **Down** and **Delete**.

"Your queries changed in another tab, so they were reloaded." means you changed them somewhere else. Try again.

## Columns

1. Choose **Columns**.
2. Tick or untick the columns under **Show these columns**.

The Hub keeps your choice for next time. **Activity Id** always shows. At first, **HQ Tags**, **Lead Ministry**, **Status** and **Translations** are hidden.

## Month and Week

Choose **Month** or **Week** to see the same filter as a calendar. An activity that runs several days shows on each of them. Use **Previous month**, **Today** and **Next month** (or **Previous week** and **Next week**) to move. Choose **List** to go back.

At most 1,000 activities fit. "Only the first 1,000 activities are shown. Narrow the filter to see the rest." means there are more.

## Watch an activity

Choose the star beside an activity to add it to your watchlist. Choose it again to remove it. Point at the star to see who else watches it, or "Nobody watches this yet".

Use **My Watchlist Only** in **Filters** to list your starred activities. The star also works during the daily change freeze.

## Excel export

1. Choose **Export and reports**. Check the filter it says it will use, such as "Uses your list filter: none (every activity you can see)."
2. Choose **Excel export**. A spreadsheet of the activities your filter finds downloads, up to 10,000.

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "More than 10,000 activities match: narrow the filter and export again" | Narrow the dates or the filter. |
| "Other exports are running: try again in a few seconds" | Wait a moment and try again. |

For the PDF reports in the same panel, see [Reports](manual:calendar-advanced-reports).

## The freeze line

"Daily change freeze." at the top of the list says when no changes can be made. While the freeze applies to you, it shows as a **Change freeze** warning and **New activity** is hidden. See [The edit lock and the daily change freeze](manual:calendar-advanced-lock-and-freeze).

## HQ tools

If you are in an HQ organization, you also see the **Confidential filter**, **Corporate Queries** under **Saved views**, and **LA status**. See [HQ review and corporate queries](manual:calendar-advanced-hq).

For **Review selected**, deleted activities, the **Exec Look Ahead** and other administrator tasks, see the Calendar administrator manual.

## Older activities

Activities that ended before the move to this Calendar weren't brought over, except awareness dates. If you need an older one, ask a Calendar administrator.

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "From must be on or before To." | Fix the dates. |
| "No activities match." | Nothing fits the filter. Widen it, or choose **Reset**. |
| "(no longer available)" in a filter box | The filter names a choice that was removed. Choose another. |
| "Couldn't save your list settings." | Your column or display choice wasn't kept. Try again. |
