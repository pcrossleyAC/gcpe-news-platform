**Who this is for:** Core administrators (Core.Admin).

You run the screens under **Administration** in the side menu. This map lists each screen, what it controls and who else notices when you change it.

## The admin map

| Screen in the side menu | What it controls | Who else it affects | Help page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Users** | Who can sign in to the Hub, and which roles they hold. | The person you change. Role changes reach them within 10 minutes. | [Users](manual:users) |
| **Calendar access** | Each person's Calendar role and ministries. | The person you change, within a minute. Calendar administrators and Calendar system administrators use this screen too, with limits that don't apply to you. | [Calendar access](manual:calendar-access) |
| **Organizations** | Ministries: names, ministers, contacts, page links, abbreviations, HQ and public settings, deactivation and hand-over. | The public site's ministry pages, subscribers, Calendar users, release editors. Site editors can edit ministry details here too. | [Organizations](manual:organizations) |
| **Organizations** > **Cabinet change** | A new cabinet's renames, new ministries and hand-overs, published together. | Everyone who uses a ministry: the public site, subscribers, staff, the Calendar, draft releases. | [Cabinet change](manual:cabinet-change) |
| **Government terms** | The dates of each government term. | The public archive, which lists releases by term. | [Government terms](manual:government-terms) |
| **Sectors, themes and tags** | The sector, theme and tag lists. | The public site, the subscribe page and the release editor, within a minute. | [Sectors, themes and tags](manual:sectors-themes-and-tags) |
| **Release page titles** | The page titles a new release can choose, such as News Release. | Release editors creating a release. | [Release page titles](manual:release-page-titles) |
| **Media list names** | Which media lists exist and their names. | News On Demand staff who manage list members, and release editors choosing lists. | [Media list names](manual:media-list-names) |
| **Error log** | Nothing: it shows the system's recent errors. | Nobody. | [Error log](manual:error-log) |
| **Website** > **Project Blue Bridge** | The public site's mourning switch. The rest of **Website** is view-only for you. | Every visitor to the public site, and every Core administrator by email. | [Website and Project Blue Bridge](manual:website-project-blue-bridge) |

When a new government takes office, you use **Government terms** and **Cabinet change** together. [When a new government takes office](topic:new-government) gives the order.

## What you see in the side menu

A Core administrator sees every screen under **Administration**. Under **Work** you see **Website** and nothing else, unless your account also holds other roles. To work on releases, subscribers or the Calendar, give your own account the role you need on **Users** or **Calendar access**.
