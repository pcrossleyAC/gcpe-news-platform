**Who this is for:** Core administrators (Core.Admin).

**Cabinet change** lets you prepare every ministry change for a new cabinet, then publish them all together, right away or at the swearing-in time. Open it from the **Cabinet change** link on **Organizations**. Nothing changes on the public site or for staff until you publish.

One cabinet change is open at a time. It saves as you go, so you can leave and come back.

## Add the changes

To merge ministries, add the new ministry first, then hand each old ministry over to it. To split one, hand it over to more than one successor. To abolish one, hand it over with no successor.

1. Under **Add a change**, choose one of:
   - **Rename or change a ministry**: pick the **Ministry**, then change its **Name**, **Abbreviation** or **Minister**. Renaming keeps the ministry's web address, subscribers and staff.
   - **Add a new ministry**: enter its **Name**, and optionally its **Abbreviation (optional)** and **Minister (optional)**.
   - **Hand over a ministry**: pick the **Ministry to hand over**, then its **Successor**. A new ministry added in this change can be the successor. Choose "No successor (abolish it)" to close it with nothing moved. The "What moves to …" boxes work as on [Organizations](manual:organizations).
2. Choose **Add this change**. You see "Added:" and a summary. The change is listed under "Changes in this cabinet change".

**Remove** beside a change takes it out. Removing a new ministry also removes the hand-overs to it, and the page says which.

## Preview

1. Choose **Preview**. It runs the whole change without saving it.
2. Read "What will change": each ministry, **Now** and **After publishing**. Then "What each hand-over moves" and "Ministries after this change".
3. If you see "Fix these before publishing", fix each problem listed, then choose **Preview again**.

When it says "No problems found. You can publish it or schedule it below.", you can publish.

## Publish now

1. Under **Publish**, choose **Publish now…**. The page asks "Publish these {n} changes now? This can't be undone in one step."
2. Choose **Yes, publish now**, or **Not yet** to go back.

You see "The cabinet change is published."

## Schedule it for the swearing-in

1. Under **Publish**, choose **Schedule…**.
2. Enter the **Date** and **Time (BC time)** under "Publish at (BC time)", usually the swearing-in time.
3. Choose **Schedule**. The page shows "Publishes on {date} at {time} Pacific."

It publishes by itself; nobody needs to be there. Until then you can still edit it, publish it now, or choose **Don't publish at that time (keep it as a draft)**. Editing a scheduled change takes it off its schedule: preview it and schedule it again.

## Start over

Choose **Discard this cabinet change…**, then **Yes, discard it**. Nothing is published.

## What happens next

- Publishing makes every change at once: all of it or none of it.
- The public Ministries page updates within a few minutes.
- Old ministry pages send visitors to their successors.
- Subscribers, staff, Calendar activities and draft releases move to their new ministries shortly after.
- If a scheduled publish can't go through, the change goes back to a draft and the page shows "This change wasn't published" with the reason. Nothing is half done. Fix it, preview again, then publish or schedule it.

## If you leave before adding a change

Each change is kept as soon as you add it. If you have filled in **Rename or change a ministry**, **Add a new ministry** or **Hand over a ministry** and not yet added it, or chosen a time and not yet scheduled it, the Hub asks before you go to another screen: "You have unsaved changes on this page. Leave anyway and discard them?" Choose **Stay** to keep working, or **Leave** to discard what you filled in. Closing or reloading the tab gets your browser's own question.

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Changed by someone else since you added this." | Someone edited that ministry after you added the change. Check it on **Organizations**, then choose **Keep this change** or **Remove**. |
| "{ministries} would both use the abbreviation {abbreviation}." | Two active ministries would end up with the same abbreviation. Change one. |
| "{ministry} is handed over more than once in this change." | Remove one of the hand-overs. |
| "{successor} is handed over in this change, so it can't also take over {ministry}'s work." | Hand the ministry straight to the final ministry instead. |
| "Choose a successor for {ministry}, or choose “No successor (abolish it)”." | The hand-over has no successor. Remove it and add it again. |
| "Nothing was published. Fix the problems listed under Preview, then try again." | Preview found problems. Fix them first. |
| "Enter the date and time to publish." | Fill in both fields before **Schedule**. |
| "Nothing was scheduled. Choose a time in the future." | The time you entered has passed. |
| "This cabinet change is no longer open. Someone else may have published or discarded it." | Reload the page to see what happened. |
