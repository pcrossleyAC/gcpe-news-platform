**Who this is for:** Core administrators (Core.Admin).

In **Website**, the one thing only you can change is **Project Blue Bridge**: the switch that puts the public site into mourning when the King dies. Everything else in **Website** is view-only for you.

## What you can and can't change in Website

You can open every **Website** tab: **Carousel**, **Emergency pins**, **Live Feed**, **What's featured where**, **Resource links**, **Files**, **Website log** and **Site settings**. You see what is live, but there are no **Save** or **Publish** buttons.

Site editors make those changes. If your work needs them, give your own account the **NRMS Site editor** role on [Users](manual:users).

**Website log** lists every change to the public site's content: who, when and what. Use it to check who changed something.

## Turn Project Blue Bridge on or off

Turn it on only with approval from the Intergovernmental Relations Secretariat (IGRS). The same steps turn it off.

1. Open **Website** > **Site settings** > **Project Blue Bridge**. The page shows "Project Blue Bridge is currently OFF." (or ON).
2. Choose the **Project Blue Bridge** switch. A box asks "Turn Project Blue Bridge on?" and repeats the warning "Do not click OK unless you have approval from IGRS".
3. Type KING CHARLES III in **Type KING CHARLES III to confirm**, in capitals, exactly.
4. Tick **IGRS has approved this change**.
5. Choose **Confirm turn on** (or **Confirm turn off**). Until steps 3 and 4 are done, the button stays greyed out.

The page then shows "Project Blue Bridge is currently ON."

The banner's wording is on **Site settings** > **Site identity**, which site editors change.

## What happens next

- The public site changes to its mourning theme, with a black banner announcing the King's death. The home page changes within a few minutes; older pages follow over the next while, newest first.
- Every active Core administrator gets an email such as "Project Blue Bridge turned ON on {site}", saying who changed it and when.
- The change is listed in **Website log**.
- Turning it off puts the site back the same way.

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Type KING CHARLES III to confirm." | The phrase was not typed exactly, in capitals. |
| "Confirm that IGRS has approved this change." | Tick **IGRS has approved this change**. |
| "Someone else changed this — reload to see their changes." | Someone else changed the switch while you had the page open. Choose **Reload** and check its state. |
