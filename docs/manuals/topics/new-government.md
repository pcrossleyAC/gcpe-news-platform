**Who this is for:** Core administrators (Core.Admin), with one part for site editors (NRMS.SiteEditor).

The Hub dates the archive, builds its pages and sends old ministry addresses to the new ones by itself. You enter one date and, where ministries change, prepare one cabinet change.

## At a glance

| When | Who | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The swearing-in date is announced | Core administrator | **Government terms** > **Start new government term**, and enter the date. |
| Before the swearing-in | Core administrator | **Organizations** > **Cabinet change**: add the renames, new ministries and hand-overs, preview them, then schedule them for the swearing-in time. |
| Swearing-in day | Nobody | The term changes over at midnight Pacific. A scheduled cabinet change publishes by itself. |
| After the swearing-in | Site editors | Minister photos and biographies on **Organizations**, and the banner on **Website** > **Site settings** > **Banner**. Update minister contact records in Media Hub by hand. |

## 1. Start the new term

Core administrators only.

1. Open **Government terms** and choose **Start new government term**.
2. Enter the **Start date (required)**, normally the swearing-in date, and choose **Continue**.
3. Read the dates in the question, then choose **Confirm**.

The start date decides where every release is listed in the public archive: releases published from that day are listed under the new term. You can fix a wrong date later with **Edit**. [Government terms](manual:government-terms) has the details.

## 2. Prepare the ministry changes

Core administrators only.

1. Open **Organizations** and choose the **Cabinet change** link.
2. Add each change: **Rename or change a ministry**, **Add a new ministry** or **Hand over a ministry**. To merge ministries, add the new ministry first, then hand each old one over to it.
3. Choose **Preview** and fix anything listed under "Fix these before publishing".
4. Choose **Schedule…**, enter the swearing-in date and time (BC time), and choose **Schedule**.

Nothing changes for anyone until it publishes. [Cabinet change](manual:cabinet-change) has the details. A single ministry can also be handed over on its own from **Organizations**; see [Organizations](manual:organizations).

## 3. On the day

Nobody needs to do anything.

- At midnight Pacific on the start date, the public archive gets a page for the new term and its current-term link points there.
- At the scheduled time, the cabinet change publishes: all of it or none of it. The public Ministries page updates within a few minutes. Old ministry addresses send visitors to their successors. Subscribers, staff, Calendar activities and draft releases move shortly after.
- If the cabinet change can't publish, it goes back to a draft. **Cabinet change** shows "This change wasn't published" with the reason. Nothing is half done: fix it and publish it.

## 4. After the swearing-in: site editors

1. On **Organizations**, choose **Edit** on each ministry and update the **Minister name**, **Minister photo URL** and **Minister biography**. Upload photos under **Website** > **Files** first. See [Organizations](manual:site-editor-organizations).
2. Take down or change any election notice on **Website** > **Site settings** > **Banner**, and publish. See [Site banner](manual:site-banner).
3. Update the ministers' contact records in Media Hub. The Hub does not change them.
