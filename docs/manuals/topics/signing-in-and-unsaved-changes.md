**Who this is for:** Everyone who uses the Hub.

## Staying signed in

While a Hub tab is open, it keeps you signed in, even when the tab sits behind other windows. Your session can still end: when you sign out in another tab or window, when your computer has been asleep or offline for a while (it can be as little as a few minutes, and is never more than an hour), when the browser has put a tab it hasn't shown for a long time to sleep, or when an administrator changes your account.

A session lasts up to 12 hours from when you signed in, however long the tab stays open. After that you sign in again.

**Sign out** ends the session in that browser, including its other tabs. You stay signed in on any other computer or browser where you signed in. If an administrator deactivates your account, you are signed out at once, and signing in again tells you "Your account is not active. Contact a Core administrator."

If the Hub says "The Hub can't check your sign-in right now. Try again in a minute.", you are still signed in and nothing on the screen is lost, but nothing can be saved or opened for now. Wait a minute and try again. The message goes away by itself once the Hub can check again.

## When your session ends

If the screen you are on has nothing unsaved, the Hub goes to the sign-in page, which says "Your session ended. Sign in to carry on where you left off." Once you sign in, you are back where you were.

If the screen has unsaved changes, it stays as it was, and a box says "Your session has ended". Nothing can be saved until you sign in again: if you choose a button that saves, the screen says "Your session has ended. Sign in again to save — your changes are still here." and the box comes back.

- Choose **Sign in again** to go to the sign-in page. Afterwards you come back to the same screen. Changes to a release's documents and to a Calendar activity are kept on this computer and come back, with "Your unsaved changes were restored." Other unsaved changes are lost.
- Choose **Stay on this page** to copy what you need first. "Your session has ended" stays at the top of the page, with its own **Sign in again**.
- Or sign in again in another tab. Come back to this tab and you can save as usual.

## Leaving a screen with unsaved changes

On every screen where you change something, the Hub asks before you leave with changes you haven't saved: "You have unsaved changes on this page. Leave anyway and discard them?" This covers the side menu, links, **Help** and your browser's **Back**. Choose **Stay** to keep working, or **Leave** to discard the changes. Closing or reloading the tab gets your browser's own question.

**Sign out** asks too: "You have unsaved changes on this page. Sign out anyway and discard them?" Choose **Stay**, or **Sign out** to discard them. Signing out yourself also discards any changes the Hub kept on this computer.

## If a screen can't be shown

If something goes wrong on a screen, you see "Something went wrong" and "This page couldn't be shown. Reload the page to try again." Choose **Reload**, or **Go to the start page**. The side menu still works. If it keeps happening, tell a Core administrator.

An address that doesn't lead to a screen shows "Page not found", with **Go to the start page**.
