**Who this is for:** Calendar read-only users (Calendar.ReadOnly).

You can see activities in the Corporate Calendar, the government-wide plan of announcements and events. You can't add, change or delete them. If an activity needs changing, ask a Calendar editor of its ministry.

## What you can do

| Where | What you do there | Help page |
|---|---|---|
| **Calendar** | Find activities, filter and save filters, see them by month or week, and star the ones you follow. | [The activity list](manual:calendar-readonly-list) |
| **Updates** | See what changed recently. | [Updates](manual:calendar-readonly-updates) |
| An activity | Read its details, files and linked releases, and see every change made to it. | [Read an activity](manual:calendar-readonly-activity) |
| **Export and reports** | Download a spreadsheet or a PDF report. | [Reports](manual:calendar-readonly-reports) |

Calendar editors also add, change and clone activities. Their manual is the Calendar editor manual. HQ advanced users and Calendar administrators have their own manuals too.

## Who sees an activity

Whether one of your ministries is an HQ organization, such as GCPE Headquarters, decides what you see.

- **In a ministry:** you see the activities your ministries lead, and the ones shared with them.
- **In an HQ organization:** you see every ministry's activities, except other ministries' ones marked **Not for Look Ahead**.

Deleted activities leave the Calendar for you.

## What you see in the side menu

Under **Work** you see **Calendar**. Its tabs are **Calendar** and **Updates**.

You may also see **Releases** or **Subscribers** if you have a role there. Those have their own manuals.

## When the list is empty

"You aren't in any ministry yet, so there are no activities to show you. Ask a Calendar administrator." means your Calendar access has no ministry. A Calendar administrator adds it.

"You don't have Calendar access. Ask a Calendar administrator." means your Calendar access was removed. You stay signed in to the Hub.

## Older activities

Activities that ended before the move to this Calendar weren't brought over, except awareness dates. If you need an older one, ask a Calendar administrator.

## Keyboard shortcut

On the activity list, ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K on Windows opens **Filters** and goes to **Search for**.

## Signing in

Choose **Sign in with Microsoft** and use your government Microsoft account. You need a Hub account first: if you see "Your account hasn't been set up. Contact an administrator.", ask a Core administrator to add you. Signing out of the Hub doesn't sign you out of Microsoft.
