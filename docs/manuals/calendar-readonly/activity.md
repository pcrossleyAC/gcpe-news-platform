**Who this is for:** Calendar read-only users (Calendar.ReadOnly).

Open an activity by choosing its title in the list or the calendar, or its number on **Updates**. It says "You can view this activity but not change it." Every field is read-only, and each checklist shows only what is ticked, or "None".

Under the heading you see its status (**New**, **Changed** or **Reviewed**), when it was last updated and by whom. A "?" beside a field explains it.

## Leave an activity

Choose **Back**. You return to where you came from:

- From the list, you see the same filter, and the activity's row is brought into view and marked, if it is among the first activities loaded.
- From **Month** or **Week**, you see the same calendar.
- From **Updates**, you see the same updates.

## Files

The **Records** section appears only when it is turned on for your Calendar. It lists the activity's files, or "No files yet." Choose a file's name to open it. Only people who can see the activity can open its files.

## Linked news releases

**BC Gov News**, at the end of the **Release** section, lists the news releases linked to the activity, or says "No releases are linked to this activity."

Each line gives the release's type, its number when it has one, its status and its date, such as "Release: Draft". The status is **Draft**, **Approved**, **Scheduled**, **Published** or **Failed**. If you also have a role in **Releases**, each line opens the release. Otherwise it is plain text.

Some categories, such as **TV / Radio** and **Awareness Day / Week / Month**, have no release, so the **Release** section is hidden.

## View changes

**View changes** lists every change to the activity, newest first. Each entry says when, who and what they did, such as "created it", "changed it", "created it as a clone", "reviewed it", "transferred it" or "cleared its LA status". A table shows each field before and after. Changes brought over from the old Calendar are marked "from legacy log".

Choose **Back to the activity** to return.

## Watch it

The star beside **View changes** adds the activity to your watchlist. See [The activity list](manual:calendar-readonly-list).

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "It doesn't exist, or you can't see it." (under "Activity not found") | Check the number. The activity may be led by a ministry you're not in, it may have been deleted, or it may be marked **Not for Look Ahead**. This is also what you see if you open an old link to an activity you can no longer see. Choose **Back to the Calendar**. |
| "Couldn't load this activity." | Reload the page. |
