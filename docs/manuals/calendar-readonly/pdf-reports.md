**Who this is for:** Calendar read-only users (Calendar.ReadOnly).

**Export and reports**, above the list, makes a spreadsheet or a PDF of the activities your list's filter finds.

## Excel export

1. Set the list's filter. See [The activity list](manual:calendar-readonly-list).
2. Choose **Export and reports**. The panel starts by saying which filter it will use, for example "Uses your list filter: Dates: 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-14 · My Watchlist Only." It warns you when something you may not have meant to keep narrows it: a **Display** choice the list keeps from last time, or an activity number in **Search for**, which leaves only that activity and ignores the dates and other filters. Change the filter under **Filters** first if that isn't what you want.
3. Choose **Excel export**. A spreadsheet of the activities your filter finds downloads, up to 10,000.

## PDF reports

1. Set the list's filter to the dates and activities you want.
2. Choose **Export and reports**, and check the filter it says it will use.
3. Choose **Look Ahead**, **30/60/90** or **Planning**. You see, for example, "Preparing your Look Ahead report…".
4. Wait a few seconds. The PDF downloads by itself and you see "The Look Ahead report has downloaded."

One report is prepared at a time. If you leave the page before it downloads, make it again.

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Look Ahead** | Upcoming activities in sections, such as Events, Speeches and Releases, Issues and Reports, and Awareness Dates. With no **To** date it covers 60 days and adds the Long Term Outlook. |
| **30/60/90** | One table for each month, from the start of the **From** month. With no **To** date it covers about 90 days. |
| **Planning** | One line for each activity: its schedule, title and summary, significance and number. |

- Reports list activities in date order, whatever the list is sorted by.
- Each report, and the Excel export, says under its heading which filter it used, for example "Filter: Dates: 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-14 · Lead Ministry: HLTH", or "Filter: none (every activity you can see)".
- A report holds only activities you can see. Deleted activities are never in it.
- Reports include awareness dates even when the list leaves them out.
- Your Look Ahead shows no Executive Summary and no NEW or CHANGED flags. Those are for HQ editors and above.
- Each activity number in a report links to the activity in the Hub.
- Each report is marked "DRAFT AND CONFIDENTIAL". Share it only with people who may see those activities.
- Reports and the Excel export still mark confidential activities "Not for Look Ahead".

## Can't do this?

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "More than 10,000 activities match: narrow the filter and export again" | Narrow the dates or the filter. |
| "Other exports are running: try again in a few seconds" | Wait a moment and try again. |
| "Too many activities match: narrow the filter and run the report again" | More than 5,000 activities. Narrow the dates or the filter. |
| "Too many days in the range: narrow the filter and run the report again" | A Look Ahead covers at most a year. Choose a nearer **To** date. |
| "Too many months in the range: narrow the filter and run the report again" | A 30/60/90 covers at most a year. Choose a nearer **To** date. |
| "Too many rows to print: narrow the filter and run the report again" | The PDF would be too long. Narrow the filter. |
| "Other reports are being prepared: try again in a few seconds" | Wait a moment and try again. |
| "The report took too long to prepare: narrow the filter and run it again." | Narrow the filter. |
| "The report couldn't be prepared. Try again." | Try again. If it keeps failing, tell your Calendar administrator. |
